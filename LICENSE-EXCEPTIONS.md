## 3. Attribution Compliance Safe Harbour

Any implementation that renders the Attribution Notice as published in
[`ATTRIBUTION.md`](./ATTRIBUTION.md) — including the reference component shipped with
the Covered Code — is deemed by Infocare Hong Kong Limited to satisfy Section 14 in full. You
do not need to seek confirmation from us.

Concretely, an implementation is within the safe harbour when **all** of the following
hold, so that "prominent display" is a measurable test rather than a judgement call:

| | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Phrase | the Attribution Phrase rendered as text |
| Mark | one of the official variants, unmodified, 18–24px tall |
| Alt text | non-empty and naming the product |
| Size | effective font size of at least 12px |
| Contrast | at least 4.5:1 against its background |
| Placement | a dedicated attribution area at the foot of the interface, present at the entry interface and kept across the ordinary screens of a session (Exhibit B, "When and Where Displayed") |
| Exceptions | the cases listed under Exhibit B, "Exceptions", need not display it |

A non-interactive notice reading "Powered by" next to the graphic image satisfies
Section 14.

**No copyright notice is required in your user interface.**

**You may substitute any official variant of the mark.** Exhibit B names one graphic
image because Section 14(b) permits us to require only one. We additionally permit you
to display any of the official variants shipped in `apps/web/public/attribution/` —
full colour, monochrome dark, or monochrome light — whichever suits your interface. All
three are the full lockup; the icon-only marks in `docs/brand/` are app icons and are
not attribution marks. We ship the variants precisely so that fitting the mark to your
theme never requires editing it. Need one we do not ship? Write to
`licensing@linksense.org` and we will make it.

**No typeface is prescribed.** The notice inherits your application's font. We ask only
that it stay legible: at least 12px effective size and 4.5:1 contrast against its
background, with opacity and scaling not used to make it effectively invisible.
