## Why we ask

Two honest reasons. It tells other organizations that real teams run this in
production, which is the question every evaluator asks first. And it tells us who to
talk to when a change would affect real deployments.

If you would rather not be listed publicly but are happy for us to know, write to
`hello@linksense.org` instead.
